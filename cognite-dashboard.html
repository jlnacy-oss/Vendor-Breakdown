Company snapshot

The clearest signal of where this market is heading: an independent industrial-AI platform just sold to a strategic incumbent (Schneider Electric) for 18x revenue, consolidating into AVEVA.

Offering: Cognite Data Fusion (unified industrial data model + knowledge graph) and Atlas AI (generative/agentic AI for industrial workflows), covering plant operations, asset management and engineering.

Key facts

Founded: 2016/2017 (Oslo, Norway)

HQ: Oslo, Norway / Austin, TX

Ownership: Being acquired by Schneider Electric for $3.1B all-cash (announced June 30, 2026), folding into AVEVA

GIP & BlackRock connections

Ownership stakes below are standard institutional/index-fund positions unless otherwise noted — that is a capital-markets fact, not evidence of an operating relationship.

Indirect exposure is coming: once Schneider Electric's ~$3.1B acquisition of Cognite closes, Cognite becomes part of a company where BlackRock is the largest shareholder (7.93% of Schneider Electric, per recent filings) — passive ownership at one remove, not a strategic relationship.

What supports the story
  • Just validated at a $3.1B exit (18x 2025 revenue) by a top-tier strategic acquirer, Schneider Electric

What to pressure-test
  • About to lose independence entirely — folding into AVEVA under Schneider Electric, with integration risk for customers and staff
