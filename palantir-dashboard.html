Company snapshot

The best-capitalized, fastest-growing name in this market by far — GAAP-profitable, ~56% revenue growth, and a fast-scaling “AIP Bootcamp” go-to-market motion pulling U.S. commercial revenue up over 130% YoY.

Offering: Four core platforms: Gotham (defense/intelligence), Foundry (commercial enterprise data integration), Apollo (deployment infrastructure) and AIP (AI Platform, launched 2023) — lets organizations deploy LLMs directly against their own operational data.

04

Sentiment

Customer sentiment
Strong, vocal reference customers (BP, PG&E, NHS) appear repeatedly in Palantir's own case studies and at its AIPCon customer events, but independent third-party review volume (G2/Gartner Peer Insights) is thin relative to its market cap — most public sentiment is analyst/investor commentary rather than software-buyer reviews.
Market & analyst sentiment
What supports the story
  • Revenue growth accelerating — 56% YoY in FY2025, guiding to 61%+ for FY2026, GAAP-profitable since 2023
  • AIP Bootcamp model converts roughly 75% of prospects into signed contracts; U.S. commercial revenue up 130%+ YoY
  • S&P 500 inclusion (Sept 2024) and a fast-growing roster of blue-chip commercial reference customers
What to pressure-test
  • Valuation is priced for perfection — among the most expensive large-cap software multiples in the market
  • Deep ties to defense/intelligence work draw recurring criticism (ACLU and others) over predictive-policing use of Gotham
  • Customer lock-in risk is real: once operations run on Foundry's ontology, switching costs are very high
Employee sentiment
High talent density and real-world mission impact are the most-cited positives; work-life balance is the most-cited negative (2.8/5 on that sub-metric) alongside opaque post-cliff compensation communication.
