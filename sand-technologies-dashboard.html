Company snapshot

Product. Symmetri — a real-time "Physical AI" control layer that models physical assets and networks and acts on them directly, rather than just surfacing dashboards or analytics. Applied across water, energy, telecom, health and government operations.

Footprint. Engineering and delivery across Silicon Valley, Paris, Romania, Latin America and Africa; registered HQ entity in Mauritius; opened a formal U.S. commercial presence with a New York launch event in June 2025.

Lineage. Grew out of a decade-plus of AI/data consulting work; merged with ExploreAI in February 2024. Distinct from the unrelated, 1980s-founded "SAND Technology" (Canada, columnar database).

Leadership. Founder/CEO Fred Swaniker — Stanford MBA, ex-McKinsey; also founder of African Leadership Group / ALX, a talent pipeline of 300,000+ trained professionals feeding Sand's hiring. TIME 100 (2019, 2023 Impact Award), WEF Young Global Leader, TED Fellow.

Positioning: Sand markets itself as the creator of a new "Physical AI" category — sensing, modeling and directly acting on infrastructure — distinct from dashboard/analytics incumbents. Palantir and C3.ai are the closest comparables by ambition; Cognite, Bentley and AVEVA are closer on the digital-twin angle specifically. Sand's edge is delivery reach into Africa and other emerging markets via the ALX talent pipeline and the Cassava GPU relationship; its risk is being a smaller, services-heavy player among better-capitalized, more differentiated platform vendors — echoed in its own C differentiation score above.
